## What this policy covers

This policy covers the Kariba Lake Connect website and everyone who uses it: visitors, guests, hotels and lodges, travel agencies, boat operators and their company members, and the captains operators register with us.

Boat operators are independent businesses. When an operator receives your booking details, the operator is responsible for how it uses them for your trip, under the same law.

## What we collect

**When you create an account (everyone):**

- your full name and email address;
- a password, which is stored only in scrambled (hashed) form by our sign-in provider;
- the kind of account you chose (guest, boat operator, hotel or lodge, or travel agency);
- your phone or WhatsApp number (required for operators and agencies, optional for guests); and
- your business name (operators, hotels and agencies).

**When you book a trip (guests, hotels and agencies):**

- the name and phone number of the person the booking is for;
- the date, time or number of days, group size and type of trip;
- any notes you write for the operator;
- the price, deposit and status of the booking, and its reference number; and
- that you accepted the booking liability waiver, with its version and the time.

If a hotel or agency books for you, it gives us your name and phone number. It must have your permission to do so.

**When you review a trip:** your overall rating, any category ratings, and your comment. Your first name and the month of the trip are added from your account and the booking.

**If you are a boat operator or company member:**

- your company's name, registration number, contact email and phone;
- the boats you list, their photos, registration numbers, prices, deposit and maintenance and operating-hours records;
- verification documents you upload: company registration, proof of boat ownership, operator licence and a director's identity document, with their labels and expiry dates, and our review decision and notes;
- the names and phone numbers of the captains you register, and which captain you assign to each booking; and
- your replies to reviews.

**If you are a hotel or travel agency:** the hotel or agency your account is linked to, and the bookings you make for guests.

**Records we create:**

- which version of our terms, privacy policy, operator agreement and booking waiver you accepted, and when, and whether you opted in to marketing messages;
- a security log of changes to accounts, bookings, listings, documents, reviews and other records, showing who made each change and when; and
- a verification status and trust score for partner accounts, set by our team.

**Collected automatically:**

- **A sign-in token** stored in your browser (see "Cookies and local storage").
- **Technical logs.** Our hosting provider, Supabase, records technical information about requests to our servers, such as IP address, browser type and time, to run and secure the service.
- **Images and fonts from other providers.** Some photographs on the website load from Unsplash (images.unsplash.com) and Pexels (images.pexels.com), and the typeface loads from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). When your browser loads them, those providers receive your IP address and browser details, as with any website you visit. We don't send them anything else about you.

We don't use analytics or advertising trackers.

## Transfers outside Zimbabwe

Our database, sign-in and file storage are hosted by Supabase in the European Union, in Frankfurt, Germany (Supabase region eu-central-1). This means your personal information is transferred outside Zimbabwe.

The Cyber and Data Protection Act allows a transfer outside Zimbabwe when the recipient's country ensures an adequate level of protection, and the data is sent only so we can carry out our own tasks. We rely on:

- the protection given by the European Union's General Data Protection Regulation, which applies to Supabase's processing in Germany;
- our agreement with Supabase, which includes data processing terms requiring it to keep the data secure and use it only on our instructions; and
- the fact that the transfer is needed to perform our agreement with you, which the Act also allows.

The photograph and font providers listed above may receive your IP address on servers outside Zimbabwe when your browser loads their content.

## How we protect it

- Every request to our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
- Access rules in our database let each person see only the records their role allows; for example, an operator sees only bookings for their own boats.
- Verification documents are kept in private storage, not public links.
- Passwords are stored only in hashed form; we can't see them.
- Changes to important records are written to a security log that can't be edited through the website.
- Our team accounts are limited to the people who need them.

No system is completely secure. Please use a strong password that you don't use anywhere else.

## Data breaches

If a security breach affects personal information we hold, we will report it to POTRAZ within 24 hours of becoming aware of it, as the Cyber and Data Protection Act and its 2024 regulations require. If the breach is likely to put your rights and freedoms at high risk, we will also tell you within 72 hours, explain what happened and what you can do. We keep a record of every breach.

## Deleting your account

You can ask us to delete your account at any time from **Account** in the menu at the top of the page.

1. **Day 0.** You confirm the request. We show you the date your account will be deleted, 30 days later.
2. **Days 1 to 30.** Nothing is deleted yet. You can still sign in, and you can cancel the deletion at any time. We remind you each time you sign in.
3. **After 30 days**, we delete or anonymise your personal information:
   - your name, phone number and business name are removed from your account, and you can no longer sign in; your sign-in record is deleted, or, where other records still point to it, emptied of your email, phone and password and permanently blocked;
   - verification documents you uploaded are deleted, and the files removed from storage;
   - captains you registered are deleted, and their names and phone numbers removed from past trips on your boats;
   - on bookings you made, your name becomes "Former guest" and your phone number and notes are removed; the reference, boat, dates and amounts are kept for tax records;
   - reviews you wrote stay published as "Former guest";
   - if you operate boats and your company has another owner on the platform, your boats pass to them; otherwise your listings are taken down;
   - names, phone numbers and other personal details are removed from the security log entries about your records.

If you still have an active booking that has not happened yet when the 30 days end, we wait until it is completed or cancelled, then delete your account.

Records of what you agreed to are kept, with only your account number, as proof of consent.
