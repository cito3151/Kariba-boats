Kariba Lake Connect is a marketplace. We show boats listed by independent operators, pass booking requests to them, and keep a record of each booking.

- **The operator provides the trip.** The operator owns or manages the boat, employs the crew, sets the price and the deposit, and is responsible for running the trip safely and as described.

## Booking a trip

1. **You send a request.** You choose a boat, the date (and the time and number of hours for an hourly trip, or the number of days), and your group size. You give the name and phone number for the booking and any notes for the operator. Hotels and agencies booking for someone else must have that person's permission to share their name and phone number with us and the operator.
2. **You accept the booking waiver.** The booking form shows the booking liability waiver, which explains the risks of a lake trip. You must accept it to send the request. We keep a record that you accepted it.
3. **The price is calculated from the operator's published rate.** The total is the hourly rate times the hours, or the daily rate times the days. The price shown is the full price of the trip. Guests pay us no booking fee.
4. **Your request is not yet a confirmed booking.** It becomes confirmed only when the operator confirms it. The operator may also decline it. Until then the boat is held for your dates so nobody else can book over them.
5. **You get a booking reference.** Every booking gets a reference such as KLC-7H3QXK. You can check the status of a booking at any time on the [Track a booking](/track) page with the reference and the phone number used for the booking.
6. **The deposit.** Each operator sets a deposit for each boat, as a percentage of the total. It is at least 20% and shown in the booking form before you send your request. Once the operator confirms, you pay the deposit to the operator to secure the boat, and the operator marks it as paid.
7. **The balance is paid on the day** of the trip, directly to the operator.
8. **After the trip** the operator marks it completed, and you can leave a review.

## Cancellations, changes and refunds

**Cancelling as a guest.** You can cancel your booking in My Trips at any time before the trip is marked completed.

- **Within 7 days of booking.** Zimbabwe's Consumer Protection Act lets you cancel a booking made online within 7 days, without giving a reason and without a penalty. If you have already paid a deposit, the operator must refund it in full within 14 days of your cancellation.
- **After that.** The operator may keep a reasonable cancellation fee from your deposit. What is reasonable depends on how much notice you gave, whether the operator can rebook the boat, and normal practice in the industry. The operator must tell you their cancellation fee before you pay the deposit.
- **No fee for death or hospitalisation.** The operator may not charge a cancellation fee if you can't take the trip because you, the person the trip was booked for, or an immediate family member has died or is in hospital.

**When the operator declines or cancels.** If the operator declines your request, no booking is made and there is nothing to pay. If the operator cancels a confirmed booking, including for weather or lake conditions that make the trip unsafe, they must refund any deposit you have paid in full, or offer you another date or a comparable boat that you are free to accept or refuse.

**Changes.** To change the date, time or group size, contact the operator. If they can't agree the change, you can cancel on the terms above.

**Refunds are paid by the operator**, because the operator holds your money. If an operator does not refund you as these terms require, tell us at support@[domain]. We will contact the operator and may suspend their listings. Nothing in this section limits any other right you have under the Consumer Protection Act.

## Commission and fees

**For guests:** you pay us nothing. The price you see is the operator's price.

**For operators:** we charge a commission on each completed booking made through the website. The commission is a percentage of the booking total. The rate, and how and when it is invoiced and paid, are set out in our partner fee schedule, which we give you in writing before your first listing goes live.

**For hotels and travel agencies:** the commission terms that apply to your account are agreed when we verify your account, recorded on your partner account and set out in the partner fee schedule.

**How the rate can change.** We set the rate to cover the cost of running the platform, and it can change with operating conditions, such as:

- fuel and transport costs;
- government and regulatory levies, taxes and licence fees;
- exchange rates and the currency in which we pay our costs; and
- our hosting, payment and staffing costs.

We will give you at least **30 days' written notice** of any change, by email to the address on your partner account. A new rate applies only to bookings confirmed on or after the date it takes effect. **It never applies to a booking that was already confirmed.** If you don't accept a change, you may close your partner account before it takes effect; commission on bookings completed before then is still due at the old rate.

At present the website does not calculate or deduct commission automatically. We invoice it separately, based on the bookings marked completed on the website.
